# Tuesday 24 November 2026 — The World on One Day (Numbers)

A one-page front page of 38 verified stories from Tuesday 24 November 2026, led by the Riyadh PIF tower drone strike, the Chen Jining probe in Shanghai, and Huawei guilty on 10 of 12 counts. The numbers angle: an 8-number strip (11 · 9 · 50,018 · +8.99% · 25 · 26 · 10/12 · 96), cards picked for their strongest figures, a chip on every story, and a numbered map legend. Drawn in code with PIL; no photographs.

Run `python3 main.py` (stdlib + Pillow only) to render `entry.png` at 1024×1024.
